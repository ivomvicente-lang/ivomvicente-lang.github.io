FAQ

How do silent disco headphones work?

Audio is sent wirelessly from DJs' systems to the headphones. Each headphone has a channel switch, so guests can pick which DJ they listen to — and everyone can dance to different music at the same time.

What's the headphone range?

Up to 150 metres from the transmitter, which comfortably covers most venues, gardens and rooftops.

How many guests can you cover?

We stock 500+ headphones and can scale up for festivals and large corporate events. Just ask for a custom quote.

Do you deliver and set up?

Yes. Delivery, setup, a full sound check and on-site support during your event are all included.

Do I need a deposit?

A refundable deposit of €100 is taken at booking and returned once all equipment is collected in good condition.

What if the weather turns bad?

We'll work with you to adapt — moving the party inside or rescheduling with no extra fees.
