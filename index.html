📖 TradeDay rules & reference tables

Evaluation. Consistency = biggest winning day ÷ total net P&L ≤ the challenge % (30% QuickPay / 45% Fast Pass). An oversized day never fails the eval — it just forces you to accumulate more total profit before you can pass. Minimum 5 days (QuickPay) / 3 days (Fast Pass).

If you finish exactly at $9,000: biggest day must be ≤ $2,700 (QuickPay 30%) or ≤ $4,050 (Fast Pass 45%).

QuickPay Funded Sim: $4,500 drawdown trails your highest intraday equity until it locks at the $150,000 starting balance — dangerous while you're inside the first $4,500 of profit (open-profit giveback eats your room). No consistency rule funded; payouts from day 1; 15 contracts / 50 micros.

Fast Pass Funded Sim: drawdown moves only at end of day; 45% consistency continues while funded (biggest day ÷ gross profit incl. payouts); 5 profitable days before payouts; $2,250 cap per cycle; starts at 4 contracts.

Mindset: a losing day extends the challenge — it should never make you raise the size of your next winning day. Don't chase; keep single days in the comfort band below and let the rule dilute itself.
