Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 August 2026

Albufeira Coworking ("we", "us", "our") operates the website ivomvicente-lang.github.io and provides coworking services at Rua do Ténis 13, 8200-186 Albufeira, Portugal. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data when you use our website and meeting room booking system.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide our services:

  • Meeting room bookings — your name and selected time slots, stored locally in your browser (localStorage).
  • Admin login credentials — email and passcode, verified locally. We do not transmit or store these on any server.
  • Contact information — if you email us via mailto links, your email address and message content are handled by your email provider, not by us.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To display and manage meeting room bookings.
  • To send payment reminders (for admin use only).
  • To operate the silent disco headphone rental booking system.

We do not use analytics, tracking cookies, advertising, or any third-party data processing services.

3. Where Data Is Stored

All booking data is stored in localStorage on your own device. This data:

  • Never leaves your browser.
  • Is not sent to any server.
  • Is automatically deleted when you clear your browser data.
  • Is subject to a 90-day auto-retention policy (old bookings are automatically removed).

4. Data Retention

  • Meeting room bookings — automatically deleted after 90 days.
  • Payment reminder data — retained until manually cleared or browser data is wiped.
  • Admin session — expires after 7 days of inactivity.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access — view all data stored about you.
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data.
  • Erasure — delete all your data ("right to be forgotten"). Use the "Clear My Data" button on any booking page, or contact us directly.
  • Portability — export your data in a readable format.
  • Object — object to processing of your data.

7. Third-Party Services

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub's privacy policy applies to hosting infrastructure. We do not use any other third-party services that process personal data.

External booking platforms (GetYourGuide, Viator, Airbnb Experiences) have their own privacy policies when you click through to them.

8. Security

We implement the following security measures:

  • HTTPS encryption for all pages (enforced by GitHub Pages).
  • Content Security Policy headers to prevent XSS attacks.
  • X-Frame-Options: DENY to prevent clickjacking.
  • No server-side data storage — all data stays on your device.
  • Admin sessions expire after 7 days.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at individuals under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Effective date" above will be revised. Continued use of our services constitutes acceptance of any changes.

11. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or to exercise your rights, contact us at hello@albufeiracoworking.com.
